Frequently Asked Questions

Is Medi AI a medical diagnosis tool?

No. Medi AI provides probabilistic insights only and does not replace a medical professional.

How accurate are the predictions?

Predictions are generated using trained machine learning models and may vary based on symptoms entered.

Is my data stored securely?

The application stores user data in local CSV files for this academic demonstration.

Can I delete my prediction history?

This feature is planned for future updates.
